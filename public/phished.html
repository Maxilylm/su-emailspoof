How to Spot Phishing Emails

  • Check the sender's email address carefully. Phishing emails often use addresses that look similar to legitimate ones but have subtle differences.
  • Be suspicious of urgent language. Phrases like "immediate action required" or "your account will be suspended" are designed to make you act without thinking.
  • Hover over links before clicking. The displayed text may differ from the actual URL destination. If it looks suspicious, don't click.
  • Don't enter credentials on unfamiliar pages. Always verify you're on the correct website before entering your username and password.
  • When in doubt, verify through another channel. Contact the supposed sender directly using a known phone number or email address.

What should I do now?

Take a moment to review the email that brought you here. Try to identify the red flags. In the future, if you receive a suspicious email, report it to your IT security team instead of clicking any links.

Remember: it's always better to ask and verify than to click and regret.
