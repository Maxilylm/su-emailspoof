PhishSim - Phishing Training Dashboard

Internal phishing awareness training tool. Create and manage simulated phishing campaigns, send spoofed emails to employees, and track who clicks. Employees who click see a training page explaining how to spot phishing.

Phished Landing Page

The "You've Been Phished" awareness page shown to employees who click simulated phishing links. Includes tips on how to recognize phishing attempts.

landing-page training

Features

• Campaign management with SMTP configuration
• 4 built-in phishing email templates (password reset, document share, IT update, package delivery)
• Mass email sending with rate limiting
• Open tracking via pixel
• Click tracking with unique tokens
• Real-time campaign statistics dashboard
• "You've Been Phished" training landing page

Quick Start

npm install && npm start

Then open http://localhost:3000 in your browser.
Set BASE_URL env var for production (used in tracking links).
